\textbf{Question}\\


Solve for $x \in \mathbb{N}$ using matrices:
$
\det\myvec{x+3 & -2 \\ -3x & 2x} = 8
$

\textbf{Solution}\\
\begin{align}
\det\myvec{x+3 & -2 \\ -3x & 2x} 
&= (x+3)(2x) - (-2)(-3x) \\
&= 2x^2 + 6x - 6x \\
&= 2x^2 \\
2x^2 &= 8 \\
x^2 &= 4 \\
x &= \pm 2 \\
x &\in \mathbb{N} \implies x = 2
\end{align}
